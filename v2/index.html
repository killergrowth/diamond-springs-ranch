Our Story

Faith, family,
and the land we love.

Diamond Springs Ranch isn't a business that decided to be a ranch. It's a ranch that decided to open its doors.

Logan grew up on this land, raised cattle at 14, and built The Cowboy Experience from the ground up. His parents Byron and Susan still manage the treehouse and the covered wagon. Everybody here has calluses.

"We're not trying to perform cowboy culture. We just live it — and now we're sharing it."

We believe the best things in life happen outside, with the people you love, away from a screen. That's not a brand promise. It's just true.

Can my kids ride? What's the age or weight limit? +
Kids of all ages are welcome on trail rides. Riders must be at least 7 years old to ride independently; younger children can ride tandem with a parent. The weight limit for our horses is 250 lbs. Everyone is welcome at the Ranch Corral Experience regardless of age.
I've never ridden a horse. Is that okay? +
Absolutely. Our guides lead every ride and our horses are selected specifically for their calm, beginner-safe temperament. We'll give you a brief orientation before you mount and stay with you the whole time. Most of our guests have little or no riding experience.
How do I book? Do you take walk-ins? +
We use FareHarbor for all online bookings — you can reserve your spot at any time. We occasionally have availability for walk-ins, but we strongly recommend booking in advance especially on weekends. Call us at (316) 303-6195 if you have questions.
What should I wear? +
Closed-toe shoes are required for trail rides — boots are preferred but sneakers work fine. Wear clothes you're okay getting a little dusty. In summer, bring sunscreen and water. In cooler months, layer up — it can get windy out in the open.
Is the treehouse or covered wagon pet-friendly? +
We love animals — but for the safety of our horses and cattle, we don't allow outside pets on the ranch property. If you have questions about special circumstances, give us a call.
Can I book the ranch for a private event or corporate outing? +
Yes — we love private events. Birthday parties, corporate team building, engagement photos, family reunions, you name it. Contact us directly at (316) 303-6195 to talk through what you have in mind and we'll put together something special.
